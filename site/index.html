How the Work Moves

Member Tribes have called, by resolution, for Tribal leadership in how energy, data infrastructure, and artificial intelligence (AI) are planned, built, and governed. The task forces carry that direction into practice: they gather public research, track policy, convene Tribal leaders and staff, and bring what they learn back to ATNI. They answer to ATNI member Tribes. Direction comes from resolutions adopted by member Tribes, and the work returns to them as drafts they can take up.

  1. Resolutions Set Direction

    Member Tribes adopt resolutions at ATNI conventions. Each task force traces its charge to one or more of them.

  2. Task Forces Do the Groundwork

    The task forces gather public research, track policy, and name the decisions in front of Tribal leaders.

  3. Leaders Convene

    The proposed Tribal Energy Summit and Working Group puts Tribal leaders, Tribal staff, and partners in the same room to hear what the task forces found and ask their own questions.

  4. Drafts Return to ATNI

    Tribal leaders draft resolutions, information requests, and statements of need, and seek sponsors to carry them to ATNI committees.

Resolutions adopted by member Tribes start the cycle again.

Four Workstreams

Three task forces and a working session. Each page below lists the resolutions behind the work and the sources gathered so far.

  • Forming

    Tribal Energy Feasibility

    Supports member Tribes in assessing energy projects, transmission, and regional electricity markets on Tribal terms. It builds on the ATNI Offshore Wind Task Force, which is currently dormant.

    Builds on Resolutions #2024-23 and #2024-35

  • Called for by resolution, 2025

    Data Centers

    Examines how data center development reaches Tribal homelands through power, water, land, and data, and what Tribal governments can decide about it.

    Grounded in Resolution #25-41

  • Established by resolution, 2026

    Artificial Intelligence

    Names the common interests of member Tribes in AI technologies, laws, and policies, including youth education and data sovereignty.

    Grounded in Resolutions #2026-039, #2026-051, and #2026-034

  • Proposed for March 2027

    Tribal Energy Summit and Working Group

    Two working days where Tribal leaders, Tribal staff, and partners turn task force findings into draft resolutions and requests. Dates and venue to be confirmed.

    Draws on all three task forces

The Resolutions That Ground the Work

ATNI member Tribes adopted each of these resolutions at convention. The links go to the adopted text.

  • Resolution#2026-051

    Supporting Responsible Governance of Youth Use of Artificial Intelligence Chatbots in Schools and Education (PDF)

    2026 Annual Convention, Snoqualmie, Washington

    Urges Tribal and public schools to set strong ethical safeguards so that AI supplements, rather than replaces, critical thinking, cultural education, language revitalization, Elders, and human relationships.

  • Resolution#2026-039

    The ATNI Energy Committee Supports the Formation of a Task Force on Artificial Intelligence (PDF)

    2026 Annual Convention, Snoqualmie, Washington

    Establishes a task force under the ATNI Energy Committee to identify the common interests of member Tribes in AI technologies, laws, and policies, and to support Tribal consultation with governments and AI companies.

  • Resolution#2026-034

    Endorsement of the Tiered Sovereign Data Framework as an ATNI-Recognized Standard for Indigenous Data Sovereignty (PDF)

    2026 Annual Convention, Snoqualmie, Washington

    Endorses the Tiered Sovereign Data Framework (TSDF) and its four data tiers. Adoption remains each Tribe’s sovereign decision.

  • Resolution#2024-35

    Calling for Tribal Leadership and Oversight in Electric Grid Governance and Electric Transmission Planning (PDF)

    2024 Annual Convention, Ridgefield, Washington

    Calls for Tribal oversight and leadership in forming and governing a Western Regional Transmission Organization, and for free, prior, and informed consent in transmission planning at every level.

  • Resolution#2024-23

    Establishing an ATNI Task Force on Offshore Wind Projects (PDF)

    2024 Midyear Convention, Canyonville, Oregon

    Creates a limited-duration task force to research offshore wind, propose policy, and align advocacy, and authorizes the Executive Board to press for a halt, meaningful consultation, and funding for Tribal engagement.

Resolution #2026-034 cites Resolution #25-41, “For the Development of an ATNI Task Force on Data Centers,” from the 2025 Annual Convention. Its adopted text will be added to the library once ATNI publishes it.

    Tribes must apply for their formula awards from the Department by December 11, 2026.

    Verified against the source. Claim ef-012.

    Boundary

    Tribes must apply to DOE for their formula awards by this date (Program Notice 26-3). Read 11/05/2026 on the agency's own page. A program fact, not an eligibility determination.

  5. Tribal Energy Financing Program (TEFP), formerly the Tribal Energy Loan Guarantee Program (TELGP)

    U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Energy Dominance Financing (EDF, formerly the Loan Programs Office), published undated. Value year 2028. Agency figure.

    The first page of the Solicitation is amended by amending the date for each of (i) “Last Part I Submission Due Date (TELGP)”, (ii) “Last Part II Submission Due Date (TELGP)”, (iii) “Last Part I Submission Due Date (TELP)” and (iv) “Last Part II Submission Due Date (TELP)” to August 31, 2028.

    Verified against the source. Claim ef-014.

    Boundary

    DOE's last submission date, not a competitive round deadline. Read 11/05/2026 on the agency's own page. A program fact, not an eligibility determination.

  6. DOE Office of Indian Energy Technical Assistance for Tribal Energy Projects

    U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Indian Energy Policy and Programs, published undated. Value year 2026. Agency figure.

    Listed as 'Ongoing' on the Office's current opportunities page. Each request is evaluated on its scope and the available budget. This is technical help, not a grant.

    Partly verified. Limit: Status read 10/29/2026 on the agency's page and not rechecked since; confirm with the agency before relying on it. Claim ef-021.

    Boundary

    Status open on 10/29/2026. The catalog holds no deadline date for this program.

  7. Weatherization Assistance Program (WAP), including Tribal grantees

    U.S. Department of Energy, Office of State and Community Energy Programs, published undated. Value year 2026. Agency figure.

    DOE allocated $325 million in WAP funds and $30 million in Weatherization Readiness Funds to 56 grantees (all states, DC and five territories) on 2025-07-08. The pages checked show no Tribal-specific FY2026 notice, so Tribal access is through state plans or a DOE direct-grant determination.

    Partly verified. Limit: Status read 10/29/2026 on the agency's page and not rechecked since; confirm with the agency before relying on it. Claim ef-022.

    Boundary

    Status open on 10/29/2026. The catalog holds no deadline date for this program.

  8. Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP), including Tribal grantees

    U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Administration for Children and Families, Office of Community Services, published undated. Value year 2026. Agency figure.

    On 2026-10-09 ACF announced the first FY2027 funding release: about $3.6 billion of regular block grant funds released to all LIHEAP grantees, equal to 90 percent of funds under the continuing resolution (Public Law 119-103, signed 2026-09-02), allocated by the annual formula update. Grantees need an approved FY2027 Model Plan to draw funds.

    Partly verified. Limit: Status read 10/29/2026 on the agency's page and not rechecked since; confirm with the agency before relying on it. Claim ef-023.

    Boundary

    Status open on 10/29/2026. The catalog holds no deadline date for this program.

  9. Indian Housing Block Grant (IHBG), formula and competitive (IHBG-COMP)

    U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, Office of Native American Programs, published undated. Value year 2026. Agency figure.

    The formula grant is distributed every year through each recipient's Indian Housing Plan. The competitive IHBG-COMP notice for FY2026 (PIH-2600-DC-0048) is forecasted on Grants.gov (forecast dated 2026-06-08, about $125 million, up to 30 awards); the FY2025 IHBG-COMP closed 2026-01-15.

    Partly verified. Limit: Status read 10/29/2026 on the agency's page and not rechecked since; confirm with the agency before relying on it. Claim ef-024.

    Boundary

    Status open on 10/29/2026. The catalog holds no deadline date for this program.

  10. Indian Community Development Block Grant (ICDBG), single purpose and imminent threat

    U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, Office of Native American Programs, published undated. Value year 2026. Agency figure.

    Two FY2026 ICDBG notices are forecasted on Grants.gov (PIH-2600-DC-0034, forecast dated 2026-05-18, and PIH-2600-DC-0023, forecast dated 2026-10-06; about $90 million estimated in each, Tribal governments listed as eligible). The FY2025 notice closed 2025-12-10. HUD's page lists ICDBG imminent threat technical assistance from 2026-07-09; the imminent threat application instructions (FR-6900-N-74) show a 2026-09-30 close on Grants.gov. A forecast's estimated dates are not deadlines.

    Partly verified. Limit: Status read 10/29/2026 on the agency's page and not rechecked since; confirm with the agency before relying on it. Claim ef-025.

    Boundary

    Status forecasted on 10/29/2026. The catalog holds no deadline date for this program.

  11. FEMA Hazard Mitigation Grant Program (HMGP)

    U.S. Department of Homeland Security, Federal Emergency Management Agency, published undated. Value year 2026. Agency figure.

    Conditional: funding becomes available only after a Presidentially declared disaster. FEMA's page directs prospective applicants to their State, Tribe or territory hazard mitigation office. The TCR registry reports that FEMA's extreme-heat guidance pages returned 'This file is unpublished' on 2026-10-29; that was not re-checked here.

    Partly verified. Limit: Status read 10/29/2026 on the agency's page and not rechecked since; confirm with the agency before relying on it. Claim ef-026.

    Boundary

    Status open on 10/29/2026. The catalog holds no deadline date for this program.

  12. USDA Community Facilities Direct Loan and Grant Program

    U.S. Department of Agriculture, Rural Development, published undated. Value year 2026. Agency figure.

    USDA's page shows 'Application Window: OPEN'; applications are accepted on an ongoing basis from October 1 through September 30, subject to funds.

    Partly verified. Limit: Status read 10/29/2026 on the agency's page and not rechecked since; confirm with the agency before relying on it. Claim ef-027.

    Boundary

    Status open on 10/29/2026. The catalog holds no deadline date for this program.

  13. Elective pay (direct pay) for clean energy tax credits, Internal Revenue Code section 6417, as changed by Public Law 119-21

    U.S. Department of the Treasury, Internal Revenue Service, published undated. Value year 2026. Agency figure.

    Elective pay remains available to Tribal governments, but Public Law 119-21 changed what it pays for. The July 4, 2026 construction-start date for wind and solar has passed, so a new wind or solar project that has not begun construction must be placed in service by 2027-12-31. The beginning-of-construction rules in Notice 2025-42 may have changed after a court decision reported in June 2026; that was not confirmed on an official page, so confirm current rules with the IRS or a tax advisor.

    Partly verified. Limit: Status read 10/29/2026 on the agency's page and not rechecked since; confirm with the agency before relying on it. Claim ef-028.

    Boundary

    Status open on 10/29/2026. The catalog holds no deadline date for this program.

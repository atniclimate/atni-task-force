  12. Elective pay (direct pay) for clean energy tax credits, Internal Revenue Code section 6417, as changed by Public Law 119-21

    U.S. Department of the Treasury, Internal Revenue Service, published undated. Value year 2026. Agency figure.

    Elective pay remains available to Tribal governments, but Public Law 119-21 changed what it pays for. The July 4, 2026 construction-start date for wind and solar has passed, so a new wind or solar project that has not begun construction must be placed in service by 2027-12-31. The beginning-of-construction rules in Notice 2025-42 may have changed after a court decision reported in June 2026; that was not confirmed on an official page, so confirm current rules with the IRS or a tax advisor.

    Partly verified. Limit: Status read 10/29/2026 on the agency's page and not rechecked since; confirm with the agency before relying on it. Claim ef-028.

  • Tribal Energy Resource Agreement notices

    The Department of the Interior posts a notice when it receives a Tribe's final proposed Tribal Energy Resource Agreement, which lets that Tribe enter energy leases and rights-of-way without further Secretarial approval.

    Federal Register. Search: "Tribal Energy Resource Agreement"

    Checked 10/29/2026: 8 results.

    Federal Register. Search: "National Interest Electric Transmission Corridor"

    Checked 10/29/2026: 34 results.

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission rule that requires regions to plan long-term transmission and allocate its costs; the docket now carries notices on compliance extensions.

    Federal Register. Search: Docket No. RM21-17-000

    Checked 10/29/2026: 9 results.

    Search Federal Register Open in Policy Sentinel

  • Alliance for Tribal Clean Energy petition at the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

    The Alliance for Tribal Clean Energy asked the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission in August 2024 to defer commercial readiness deposits and exempt Tribal energy developers from withdrawal penalties in the generator interconnection queue.

    Federal Register. Search: Docket No. RM24-9-000

    Checked 10/29/2026: 2 results.

    Search Federal Register Open in Policy Sentinel

  • Clean energy tax credit payments for Tribal governments

    The Treasury Department and the Internal Revenue Service decide how Tribal governments and their wholly owned entities claim clean energy credits as direct payments; a final rule on those entities published in December 2025.

    Federal Register. Search: "elective payment" "Indian Tribal government"

    Checked 10/29/2026: 16 results.

    Search Federal Register Open in Policy Sentinel

  • Pacific offshore wind lease sale notices

    The Bureau of Ocean Energy Management published the notices for the Pacific wind lease sales off California and Oregon; the Affiliated Tribes of Northwest Indians formed an Offshore Wind Task Force on this subject in 2024 (Resolution 2024-23).

    Federal Register. Search: "Pacific Wind Lease Sale"

    Checked 10/29/2026: 6 results.

    Search Federal Register Open in Policy Sentinel

  • Bonneville Power Administration rate and tariff notices

    The Bonneville Power Administration sets wholesale power and transmission rates for much of the Northwest, and its Federal Register notices announce each rate proceeding and public hearing.

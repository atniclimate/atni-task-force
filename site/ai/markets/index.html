AI

How much of the market rides on AI, and how the money loops

Seven companies account for a large share of the stock market benchmarks that pension funds, endowments, and Tribal investment portfolios track: Apple, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Tesla. This page is a dated record of how those companies moved and how the arrangements between them work, not investment advice and not a forecast.

11/17/2026 Sources checked Sources for this page

Questions this page answers

How much of the market rides on seven companies?

About 38.3 percent of the Nasdaq-100 fund on 11/16/2026, and about 33.9 percent of the S&P benchmark's market value on 10/30/2026.

What moves when AI news breaks?

On 11/05/2026, NVIDIA moved -9.3 percent at the close, the Nasdaq-100 -6.4 and the S&P benchmark -4.4.

What is circular financing, and what holds it up?

A seller invests in or backs a buyer that commits to buy its chips or cloud; officials name the pattern as a risk, and signed contracts and growing revenue have held it up so far.

What might a council or treasurer ask?

These are questions, not advice.

  1. Who stands behind a tenant's payments if the tenant cannot pay? NVIDIA's filing shows guarantees capped at 105 billion dollars on one campus; the guarantees end once OpenAI reaches a satisfactory credit rating.

  2. How is the project financed, and what happens to the lease if that financing changes?

  3. How many lease years are covered by a signed customer commitment?

  4. How much of a Tribe's invested funds rides on the same seven companies? Seven companies made up 38.33 percent of the Nasdaq-100 fund on 11/16/2026.

  5. What notice or step-in rights does the agreement give? This is a question for counsel.

The Seven on 11/16/2026

The "Magnificent Seven" is a press nickname that Bank of America strategist Michael Hartnett gave the group in 2023, after the 1960 Western. The seven are Apple, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Tesla.

NVIDIA's Change of Chief Executive

On 11/11/2026, the board of directors accepted Jen-Hsun Huang's resignation as president, chief executive officer, and director, effective immediately. Colette M. Kress, the chief financial officer, is interim chief executive. Harvey C. Jones is chair. The filing states no reason.

NVIDIA shares fell 9.3 percent on 11/05/2026, in a broad selloff, and 4.34 percent on 11/12/2026. The company's third-quarter report was scheduled for 11/17/2026; every NVIDIA figure on this page predates it. See the day's moves.

How the Money Loops

Why Analysts and Officials Call It Fragile

The IMF, the Bank of England, and the Federal Reserve's own survey have named the same pattern: circular financing, concentrated debt, and backlog that depends on the same companies that supplied the capital.

IMF (October 2026). Staff reviewed fourteen deals since 2023: about 300 billion dollars of equity, 650 billion dollars of debt, and 1.4 trillion dollars of commitments. If key investees falter, the IMF wrote, equity and debt lose value "precisely when future revenue streams tied to them are impaired," a pattern it calls "wrong-way risk."

Third-quarter spending against operating cash. Amazon spent 77.3 billion dollars on property and equipment against 42.4 billion dollars of operating cash. Meta spent 33.9 billion dollars against 32.8 billion dollars. Alphabet reported negative free cash flow of 9.1 billion dollars.

Bowman (November 2026). Lease and guarantee payments could add "more than three times" the debt, an estimate she cited.

Hyperion. Meta's Louisiana campus carries about 27 billion dollars of development costs. A Brookings conference draft found it about 90 percent debt, with a debt service coverage ratio of 1.12 times.

Lucent. At 09/30/2000, the company had committed up to 6.7 billion dollars of customer credit.

Officials. The Bank of England (September 2026) said "circular arrangements" in AI financing "could complicate the assessment of risks and could amplify losses if expectations disappointed." The Federal Reserve's November 2026 survey found that respondents widely cited the risk of a sharp correction in AI-related equities; these are respondents' views, not the Fed's forecast.

Why It Has Held So Far

Signed backlog, growing revenue, financially sound investors, and rising construction have held it up so far.

Backlog. Oracle reported remaining performance obligations of 664 billion dollars, about 13 percent recognized within twelve months. Microsoft's commercial backlog grew 84 percent to 678 billion dollars, 25 percent without OpenAI.

Revenue. Anthropic said its run-rate revenue crossed 47 billion dollars in May 2026. The round that followed counted 15 billion dollars of previously committed hyperscaler investments inside it.

Investors. The IMF noted that the investors are "generally financially sound." Microsoft covered its third-quarter spending from operations: 39.367 billion dollars of property and equipment additions against 58.0 billion dollars of operating cash.

Construction. Private U.S. data center construction ran at a 96.462 billion dollar annual rate in September 2026, up 90.3 percent from September 2025.

Two estimates of AI's share of growth. J.P. Morgan Asset Management's proxy put AI-related investment at 0.47 percentage points of 2.1 percent growth, a strategist's estimate. ING's economist called it "a third" of 2026 growth, an opinion.

Where Retellings Differ from the Record

Each retelling below is set beside what the record says.

The 100 billion dollars was a letter of intent. NVIDIA's 10-Q shows guarantees capped at 105 billion dollars, a different instrument.

Apple, at 8.73 percent of the Nasdaq-100 fund, outweighs NVIDIA at 6.38 percent.

In the third quarter, of the four companies here, only Microsoft's operating cash covered its spending: 58.0 billion dollars against 39.4 billion dollars. Amazon, Meta, and Alphabet each spent more than they generated, as the third-quarter figures above show.

"More than 1 trillion dollars" is CNBC's tally of one session's loss.

Oracle's filing reports 664 billion dollars of remaining performance obligations and names no customer.

What the Marks Mean

Every figure on this site carries a claim id and one of two statuses.

  • Verified, a full square: the figure was checked against its source.
  • Partly verified, a half-filled square: the source supports the figure with a limit, and the limit goes with the figure, in the plate's Reading, its card, and its Sources page.

On a plate, a filled mark is measured or reported and a hollow one is modeled; a hatched bar runs past the plate's scale, with its figure printed.

Terms

The words the pages link here, as this site uses them.

aMW
Average megawatts: a year's electricity use stated as the steady power that would use it evenly, hour by hour. It is less than a load's peak MW whenever the load factor is below one.
CO2e
Carbon dioxide equivalent: greenhouse gases stated as the amount of carbon dioxide that would warm the climate as much.
load factor
A load's average draw as a share of its peak. A data center that runs near full power all year has a high one.
modeled
Worked out from published inputs and stated assumptions rather than measured; a hollow mark on a plate.
MW
Megawatts: a rate of electric power, the size of a load or a power plant at full draw. A thousand kilowatts.
partly verified
The source supports the figure with a stated limit, and the limit travels with the figure wherever it appears.
PUE
Power usage effectiveness: a data center's total energy divided by what its computers use. The rest goes to cooling, power conversion, and lighting; closer to one is leaner.
the Council
The Northwest Power and Conservation Council, which plans the region's electric power under the Northwest Power Act.
TWh
Terawatt-hours: an amount of electric energy, a billion kilowatt-hours, the scale of a region's or a country's use in a year.
Wh
Watt-hours: an amount of energy at the scale of one device, such as a phone charge or an hour of streaming on a screen.
